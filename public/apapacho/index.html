Mexican register throughout, the Spanish your family actually speaks. Anything marked aguas is fine with friends and wrong in front of somebody's abuelita. For volume listening: Dreaming Spanish (comprehensible input, start at your level), then the Andrea la Mexicana podcast for pure Mexican ear.

Signs live in motion; these notes are memory aids, not the teacher. One real thing to know early: in ASL your face is grammar, eyebrows up for yes/no questions, eyebrows down for wh-questions.
