NIGHT BUS

a scene · est. may 2026 · the bell rings friday 19:00 · no floor, cap 150, fork after

this is the model town: invented people, real structure. it stands here until real scenes move in, and it is the template anyone can copy. nothing on this page is a person.

ANYONE CAN KNOCK

vouching may be selective; reachability may not. every scene owes the world one visible door, and this page is ours. a scene with no door is a clique, and cliques are not scenes under the charter.

to knock: say who you are, what you make, and which of us you've met. or come to a show first; friday works.

how you'd get in: names staked on you, two of them or half the scene, whichever is smaller. the vouchers are listed on your room for as long as you're here. pseudonyms are the assumed case, and nobody will ever ask for your documents or your face.

who belongs here is not published. seven of us, and the roll stays with the scene by default. a membership list is a thing that gets used; the door is public, the roster isn't.
