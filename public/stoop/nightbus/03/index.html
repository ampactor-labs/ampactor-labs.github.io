WALKING HOME FROM THE SUBSTATION SHOW

essay · moss

the sodium lamps on Halsey are being replaced by LEDs, block by block, and you can stand on the seam: orange behind you, white ahead. after the show everyone walked the orange direction, no one deciding it out loud. vera says the hum is the same note as the substation, F, and that the band tuned to it on purpose. i believe her lie completely. a city is a scene that got too big to fit in a basement, and it forked, and forked again, and forgot it ever had a commons.
