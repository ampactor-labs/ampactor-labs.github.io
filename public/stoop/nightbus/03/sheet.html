PRINT IT

letter · landscape · scale 100% · margins none.

honesty clause: printers and folding hands vary. print ONE test sheet, fold it, check the pages read 1→8. wrong order? swap layout and reprint. then print the run.

FOLD IT

  1. Fold in half the long way. Crease hard. Unfold.
  2. Fold in half the short way, then each end back to the middle. Unfold: eight panels.
  3. Fold the long way again. Cut along the crease from the folded edge, across the middle two panels only.
  4. Unfold. Fold the long way, slit on top. Push both ends toward the middle; four wings make a plus sign.
  5. Collapse the wings around one panel and crease it into a book. The cover lands on front.

the top row prints upside-down on purpose. that's not a bug, that's the fold. staples optional, one in the spine if you're feeling official.

Walking home from the substation show

essay · moss

the sodium lamps on Halsey are being replaced by LEDs, block by block, and you can stand on the seam: orange behind you, white ahead. after the show everyone walked the orange direction, no one deciding it out loud.

vera says the hum is the same note as the substation, F, and that the band tuned to it on purpose. i believe her lie completely.

a city is a scene that got too big to fit in a basement, and it forked, and forked again, and forgot it ever had a commons.
