STOOP /stoop/ — scene host

YOUR GROUP CHAT DESERVES A ZINE.

folk media at scene scale. mass media was made by industries for audiences; social media industrialized us, one tiny broadcaster each. this is the third thing: made by the people in it, for the people in it, small like a scene, periodic like a zine, pointed at the physical world.

there is no feed anywhere in this system. that is the load-bearing refusal.

TWO PEOPLE IS A SCENE

there is no minimum size, and there never will be. you and one other person publishing on a rhythm is the whole thing working: two people ship on a deadline, eight people with no habit miss the first bell. vouching scales down to a founding pair, the editor's chair alternates instead of rotating, and a scene with no costs owes no dues.

add person three when person three can be handed a back issue. that is a completely different ask than joining a blank thing.

EVERY ISSUE HAS AN ADDRESS

because a zine you can't hand to somebody isn't doing its job.

/stoop/<scene>/   the scene's public door, and its shelf
/stoop/<scene>/03/   issue №03, reading view
/stoop/<scene>/03/sheet   the same issue, imposed for a printer
/stoop/<scene>/latest/   alias for the newest one

numbers, not slugs: titles get argued about, the number is the spine. links inside a scene are relative, so the whole directory copies to another host, a thumb drive, or a tarball and nothing breaks. this host is a mirror. the scene's canonical form is its own repository, and the day a scene wants its own domain the move is a copy and a DNS record.

LIVE HERE

night bus — the model town. invented people, real structure. it stands until real scenes move in, and it is the template anyone can copy.

read issue №03, "power cuts", then fold the same issue onto one sheet of paper. same words, two substrates, neither one the real one.

START ONE

stage 0 is paper and hand tools; no software gets built until somebody's issue №02 ships. that gate is the whole growth policy.

the press kit: one letter sheet, eight pages, one cut, with fold instructions and a test-sheet mode. print it, fill it, hand it to somebody.

the build: rooms, scenes, the desk, the rounds, the law — sixteen views in one 55 KB file that makes zero requests to anybody.

the repo: charter, design, the stage 1 spec. AGPL and CC BY-SA. copy it; that's what it's for.
